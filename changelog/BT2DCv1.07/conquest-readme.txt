BfME2 Conquest Mode by MaDDoX
Last Revision: 28/1/07 (v1.2)

1. Introduction

- Conquest mode is a special gameplay mode for the PC real-time strategy game The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-Earth II by Electronic Arts. It's a map-based game mode, which means it's automatically activated whenever a conquest mode map is chosen and the game starts, without altering the default gameplay on all regular maps.

2. Objectives

- The main objective of the Conquest Mode is increasing the strategic depth of the game, adding the traditional economic control points element.
- Restoring and improving the 'implicit resource' design from the first The Battle for Middle Earth. 'Implicit Resources' means that instead of nominal or explicit resources like gold, metal, wood, as in most RTS games, multiple resources are mimicked by discounts given by specific structures to certain unit types, with the management simplicity of a single explicit resource.
- Speeding up the early game action by means of starting battalions for each faction, which also increase the starting mixing possibilities since you already start with one unit type

Rules:

- You start with one porter (not 2) and starting forces depending on faction. Men: 2 soldiers; Elves: 1 lorien archer; Dwarves: 1 phalanx; Mordor: 2 orcs; Isengard: 1 uruk-hai; Goblins: 2 goblin warriors
- Regular Resource Buildings (Farm, Furnace, etc) cost 500 and only give 10 cash per 6 seconds load at 100% efficiency. Lumbermills cost 800.
- All CPs (control points) are unattackable, have a small public line of sight around their flags, and when captured for the first time give you 200 bonus credits. There are three CP types:
  -> Aitheon - Ancient Temples of devotion to the angelic creatures called Ainur, these structures increase your army faith, a great encouragement to your soldiers training.
	- 18 resources/6s load; Infantry Blessing upgrade (50 cost) reduces soldier and pike costs (15%/25%/30%/35% for 3/4/5/6+ upgrades respectively); builds porters and heroes, collects the one ring
  -> Workpit - Reminiscent structures from a long forgotten Hobbit civilization, Workpits are told to have been enchanted to produce iron, hence massively improving artillery and all equipment production.
	- 16 resources/6s load; lava forge upgrade (150 cost) discounts all upgrades costs (15%/25%/30%/35% for 1/2/3/4+ upgrades respectively) and machines cost
  -> Vardarin - Some believe these statues of devotion have a mystical connection to Varda herself. All plantation around Vardarins grow unexplanably fast, making breeding of animals really easy.
	- 16 resources/6s load; varda blessing upgrade (150 cost) discounts animals (monsters/cavalry) and resource building costs (15%/25%/30%/35% for 1/2/3/4+ upgrades respectively); 500 line of sight
- Captured CPs lose their upgrades, but not their experience
- Each CP has an identifying symbol in the minimap - (·) Aitheon; (^) Workpit; (v) Vardarin
- Resource increasing powers (industry and dwarven riches) work on all CPs.
- All resource buildings in conquest mode automatically get +25% and +50% production rates at level 2 and 3. The experience levelup requirements are equal to the furnace.

Map List:

[1v1] Fords of Isen (Conquest) - Classic 1v1 map with decent balance. Middle creeps are removed in this conquest version, to lessen a bit the focus on early creeping.
[1v1] West Emnet (Conquest) - Port from this excellent 1v1 map from BfME1. CPs replace the former "Settlements" exactly, with some stronger creeps on corners to allow for fast hero leveling strats.
[1v1] East Rohan (Conquest) - Another BfME1 1v1 map port, this one with no "easy CP" near fort. CPs replace the former "Settlements" exactly, with spider lairs in place of the middle warg creeps.
[2v2] Anorien (Conquest) - Port from the most played 2v2 map on BfME1. CPs replace the former "Settlements" with some adjustments, a couple warg creeps added on the far center area.
[2v2] Druadan Forest (Conquest) - Port from this excellent 2v2 map from BfME1. CPs replace the former "Settlements" exactly, with some new creeps (spiders) on corners to allow for faster hero leveling.
