ANKH-MORPORK (Bt2DC Version)
----------------------------

Based on Beta 1.0 version created by YidArmy1885

Balance and Flow Redesign by MaDDoX (www.gamereplays.org)

----------------------------

Ankh-Morpork was the rather hedonistic city which infamously burnt down in Terry Pratchett's first Discworld novel. The map features the smoldering ruins of a city in the southeast corner. Mountainous terrain in the northwest is traversed via a mountain pass. A river divides the map's southern and northern halves. Three wargs, one wight and one cave troll for creeping. Two Inns available for capture, along with a Signal Fire.

Enjoy the map!
